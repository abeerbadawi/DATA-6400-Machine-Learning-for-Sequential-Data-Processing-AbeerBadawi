# Research Project and Proposal

The course research project is structured as an **ACL-style LLM research study** centered on sequential language data. Students are expected to move from a clearly motivated research question to a feasible experimental design, rigorous evaluation, and a defensible research contribution.

## Proposal Expectations

The approximately two-page proposal should include:

1. A concise research title and team information.
2. A clearly defined problem involving natural language as sequential data.
3. A research question or hypothesis motivated by a gap in current LLM research.
4. A short related-work discussion grounded in ACL/EMNLP/NAACL literature.
5. A justified dataset choice, including data format, labels or targets, preprocessing, and limitations.
6. An LLM-based methodology, including the role of sequence length, context, positional structure, or chunking where relevant.
7. Explicit compute requirements: GPU type, number of GPUs, memory, estimated training/inference time, and local/cloud assumptions.
8. An evaluation plan with quantitative metrics, baselines, qualitative/error analysis, robustness, and/or generalization experiments.
9. A clear statement of novelty and 2–3 expected research outputs.
10. ACL-style references.
